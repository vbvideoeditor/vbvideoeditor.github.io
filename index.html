About Me

Personal, detailed and emotion-focused editing.

I’m a wedding video editor from Ahmedabad, Gujarat, working solo under the name VB Video Editor. My focus is on making your wedding footage look polished, cinematic and full of emotion – without losing the natural moments of your big day.

I handle everything from traditional long videos to cinematic highlights, teasers and reels. You send me the footage; I send you clean, colour-corrected, well-timed edits that you’ll love to rewatch and share with family and friends.
